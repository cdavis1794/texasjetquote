Austin airport choice

Compare AUS and EDC by total trip fit

Ask the provider to quote the complete trip from both airports when aircraft availability allows. A small mileage difference can be outweighed by positioning, fees, runway fit, or ground time.

AUS

Commercial airport + general aviation

Austin-Bergstrom International

Often practical when passengers are connecting to airline service or prefer the southeast Austin area. General-aviation availability, handling, fees, and ramp access must be confirmed for the date and aircraft.

EDC

Dedicated general aviation airport

Austin Executive Airport

Northeast of central Austin and designed around general aviation. It can be convenient for private-flight access, subject to aircraft performance, runway suitability, current conditions, and the ground itinerary.

Austin charter FAQ

Questions to settle before requesting aircraft

Which Austin airport is best for a private jet charter?

AUS and EDC can both be viable. Compare the complete itinerary: passenger ground time, current aircraft position, runway suitability, handling, fees, and any connection to airline service.

How much does an Austin private jet charter cost?

The total depends on route, aircraft, positioning, schedule, airport fees, crew requirements, and trip structure. Use the planner for an editorial range, then request current availability and written terms for the actual itinerary.

Who provides the aircraft and final pricing?

TexasJetQuote is an independent Villiers affiliate and does not operate aircraft. The ranges shown here are non-binding planning estimates. Current aircraft availability, operator identity, final pricing, and terms are provided through Villiers and the certificated operating carrier.

What should I include in an Austin charter request?

Include origin and destination, departure date and time window, passenger count, baggage, pets, accessibility needs, flexibility, and whether the trip is one-way, same-day return, or multi-day.
