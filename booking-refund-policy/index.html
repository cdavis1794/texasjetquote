Before you pay

Booking, cancellation and refund policy

TexasJetQuote does not accept flight payments or control reservations.

Last updated: August 5, 2026.

TexasJetQuote does not sell flights

The website provides information and referral links. It does not take charter payments, issue tickets, confirm aircraft or hold funds. Therefore, TexasJetQuote cannot cancel a booking or issue a flight refund.

Your provider’s written policy controls

Any booking, payment, change, cancellation or refund is governed by the written agreement between you and the provider, broker and/or legal operator involved. Terms can vary by itinerary and may include nonrefundable amounts, cancellation windows, weather provisions, aircraft substitutions and passenger-delay charges.

Before payment

  • Identify the provider and legal operator
  • Read the complete cancellation and refund section
  • Confirm the total expected charges and payment recipient
  • Save the quote, contract, receipt and support contact

If you need help with an existing trip

Contact the company named on your contract or receipt. TexasJetQuote cannot access third-party booking records.
