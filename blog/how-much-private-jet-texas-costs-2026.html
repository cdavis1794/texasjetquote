Texas Private Jet Costs at a Glance (2026)

In 2026, chartering a private jet in Texas costs $3,000 to $18,000 per hour depending on aircraft size. Most Texas business travelers fly light to midsize jets at $4,200-$8,500/hr. The good news: Texas has more empty legs than any other state, so savvy flyers rarely pay full retail.

The Texas Advantage

With 28 commercial-service airports and 180+ Part 135 operators based in Texas, repositioning flights are constant. DFW↔IAH alone sees 40+ empty legs per month. That supply is why Texas empty legs average 66% off retail vs 50% nationally.
